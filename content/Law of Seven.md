The [[Law of Three]] and [[Law of Seven]] are among the most insightful teachings by [[George Ivanovich Gurdjieff|Gurdjieff]] (and later [[P. D. Ouspensky]], see [[In Search of the Miraculous]] and [[The Fourth Way]]).

Together with the [[Law of Three]] it gives rise to the [[Enneagram]] which in turn lines up with the 8 [[Rasa]]s found in [[Rajanaka Abhinavagupta]]'s commentary on the [[Natya Shastra]] (the [[Abhinavabharati]])
