Landmark book by [[George Boole]] published in 1854.

[[Boolean Algebra]] presents binary choices: Something is either True or False

In reality, however, there is always the underlying relation which presents the two alternative options in the same context in the first place, thus often we should consider a neutral position in between (such as in [[Ternary Logic|Trinary Logic]])
